# Deliverable patterns

Select and adapt the smallest pattern that satisfies the request. Do not force every heading into every document.

## Evidence synthesis

Use: question and scope; search boundary; evidence table; synthesis; disagreement or gaps; applicability; conclusion; references.

Keep source findings separate from the author's interpretation. If the search was not systematic, describe it as a targeted search rather than a systematic review.

## Clinical or technical rationale

Use: purpose; available inputs and quality; relevant findings; integrated interpretation; alternatives or disagreement; uncertainty and missing information; proposed conclusion or route; item requiring human review; references.

The rationale should expose the evidence and decision logic at a useful summary level. It should not claim to reveal hidden chain-of-thought.

## Method, protocol, or rule

Use: purpose and scope; definitions; inputs and eligibility; roles and authority; ordered method; decision table or workflow; failure behavior; required output; validation; provenance/version; references.

Distinguish implemented behavior, validated behavior, planned behavior, and hypotheses.

## AI study document

Use: research question and intended use; data provenance and cohort; reference standard; model and comparator; evaluation design; metrics and uncertainty; subgroup/external validation; error analysis; limitations; governance/reproducibility; references.

Do not describe held-out internal performance as external validation or retrospective performance as demonstrated clinical utility.

## Supervisor brief

Keep the written brief to roughly one page unless requested otherwise:

1. **Decision/question:** one sentence stating what feedback or authorization is needed.
2. **Context:** only the facts necessary to understand the problem.
3. **Key evidence:** three to five findings, preferably in a compact table.
4. **Interpretation:** the most defensible conclusion and why.
5. **Uncertainty/alternatives:** material limitations and credible competing explanations.
6. **Proposed next step:** an actionable recommendation or scoped question.

For oral delivery, provide a 30-second summary followed by optional two-minute supporting points. End with the specific question for the supervisor; do not end with a generic request for comments.

## Translation package

When traceability matters, provide the translated document plus a short terminology/semantic-change note. Record deliberate audience adaptations, ambiguous source phrases, and any term retained in the source language. Do not clutter the main document with translation commentary unless requested.
